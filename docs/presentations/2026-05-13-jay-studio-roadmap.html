Your Feedback

What we heard

From our May 7 conversation — the themes that shaped this roadmap.

  • ●Apple-like simplicity — hide technical complexity, focus on the creative process
  • ●Image morphing — select 2-6 images, AI morphs between them with per-transition control
  • ●Segment re-rendering — redo just one bad segment without regenerating everything
  • ●Upload your own images — bring personal work into the studio
  • ●Better image models — Flux, OpenAI alongside existing models
  • ●Image variations — MidJourney-style alternatives, not just same-prompt reruns
  • ●Polished aesthetic — a tool that feels as good as the art it creates

Phases 3 & 4

Models & Variations

Expand the image generation toolkit, then add MidJourney-style variations.

Phase 3: New Image Models

  • Flux (Black Forest Labs) — closest open-source MidJourney competitor
  • OpenAI (gpt-image-1) — high quality, different aesthetic
  • Added to existing model picker dropdown
  • Same pattern as Z Image Turbo integration

Phase 4: Image Variations

  • Right-click any keyframe → "Generate Variations"
  • 4-8 alternatives in a mini-grid
  • Click to swap into the flow
  • Uses img2img from Flux / OpenAI
  • Avoids repetitive artifacts from same-prompt reruns
10

Design

UI Direction

Not a separate phase. Every feature ships looking like this.

Progressive Disclosure
Start with nothing. Reveal controls as they become relevant. Advanced settings behind an expandable "brain icon" — most users never see inference steps or guidance scales.
Dark & Minimal
Dark theme, generous spacing, refined typography. No cramped controls. The interface recedes so the art is the focus. Pika-level polish.
Smooth Motion
Fade/slide animations for progressive reveal. Settings don't pop in — they ease in. Every interaction feels intentional and crafted.
Two Modes
Flow (default) for most creators. Batch (Advanced) for power users who want the combinatorial grid. Same design language, different complexity.
